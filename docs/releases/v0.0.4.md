Read aloud arrives: any book in the system's voices or in neural voices
that download in Settings, plus pronunciation on word cards, live reload
and a lighter app. This release also gathers everything since v0.0.3.

## What's new

- **Read aloud.** Any book reads aloud from where you right-click or
  press the shortcut. Each sentence lights up as it's read, and pages turn
  by themselves. The system's own voices work with no download. Pick the
  voice, speed, pitch and volume in Settings › Read aloud, and hear a
  sample before you choose.
- **Neural voices.** Three voice models download in Settings and run on
  your computer, with nothing sent anywhere:
  - **Kokoro:** natural voices in English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese,
    French, Hindi and Mandarin. It comes in Full, Half and Small builds,
    so you can trade size for quality.
  - **Piper:** quick on any computer, with eleven English voices.
  - **Kitten:** the lightest, from 24 MB, with eight English voices.

  Voices share their pronunciation data on disk. A model that hasn't
  been used for a while unloads, and its memory goes back to the system.
- **How words sound.** Dictionary and AI cards show an English word's
  pronunciation, American, British or both, as IPA or an easy respelling.
  A speaker button says the word aloud. The pronunciation data is a
  download in Settings › Vocabulary and shares files with the voice
  models.
- **Books reload when saved elsewhere.** Edit a note, re-export a PDF or
  rebuild an EPUB in another app, and the open book reloads by itself
  within about two seconds, staying where you were reading.
- **A book's own design.** EPUB chapters keep their boxes, margins and
  rules. Pages fill the pane in Single and Two pages views. Settings are
  grouped into folds.
- **AI settings in two tabs.** AI holds what the AI does: the AI
  Dictionary, the library organizer and page tasks. AI models holds the
  models, with each provider's logo and what each model is used for.
- **One kind of dialog.** Every sheet and alert is a centred card over
  the same backdrop.
- **Notifications stack in the corner.** They pile up bottom-right. Click
  to open the pile, swipe one away or clear them all. Repeats are counted
  instead of piling up.
- **Search as a capsule** over the page, with a map of its hits.
- **Lighter.** Closed books let go of their memory, paragraphs take
  about 40% less room, a long book closes about 30 MiB lower, and the
  download is smaller.

## Fixes

- **Windows shows Mareader's icon** in Explorer, the Start menu, desktop
  shortcuts, pinned taskbar buttons and Installed apps, instead of the
  generic program icon. The setup program shows it too.
- Imported Markdown notes keep their pictures, including ones from
  folders above the note and ones written as HTML `<img>` tags, even
  after the original is gone. Removing the book removes them.
- Files you pick or drop are always copied into the library, and books
  copied in before are no longer treated as read in place.
- Highlights, selections and search matches in reflowed books are as tall
  as the letters, not the whole line.
- With the sidebar floating, the title bar's buttons no longer open it by
  accident.
- Changing the type keeps your reading place.

## Which file to download

| System | File | Install |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon | `Mareader_0.0.4_aarch64.dmg` | open it and drag Mareader to Applications |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `Mareader_0.0.4_x64.dmg` | the same |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `mareader_0.0.4_x64-setup.exe` | run it; it installs for you alone, no administrator needed |
| Windows, managed machines | `mareader_0.0.4_x64_en-US.msi` | `msiexec /i` it, or deploy it as usual |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS | `mareader_0.0.4_amd64.deb` | `sudo apt install ./mareader_0.0.4_amd64.deb` |
| Arch and its family | `mareader_0.0.4_x86_64.tar.gz` and `PKGBUILD` | put both in one folder, then `makepkg -si` |
| Any other Linux (glibc 2.35 or newer) | `mareader_0.0.4_x86_64.AppImage` | `chmod +x` it, then run it |

Installing over v0.0.3 keeps your library, settings and highlights. On
Windows, a shortcut made by v0.0.3 may keep the generic icon until it is
made again, or until Windows refreshes its icon cache.
`SHA256SUMS` lists every file's checksum: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
--ignore-missing` on Linux, `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`
on a Mac.

## The first launch

This build is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so each system asks
once:

- **macOS:** "Apple could not verify Mareader". Choose Done, then open
  System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway next to
  Mareader. Later launches open directly.
- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run
  anyway.
- **Linux:** nothing to approve. The AppImage only needs to be executable.

## Known limits

- Neural voices are downloads of 24 MB to 327 MB each. The system voices
  need none. On Linux, the system voices go through Speech Dispatcher.
- Live reload follows files read in place. Books copied into the library
  don't change.
- Your library stays on this computer; syncing between machines comes in
  a later release.
- Linux packages are for x86_64 only; ARM Linux and Windows on ARM have
  no build yet.

Before publishing, CI built every installer on its own system, then
installed each one, opened a book with it and removed it again.
