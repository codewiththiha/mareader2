Reading aloud becomes reading along: the words light up as they're
heard, a new player sits over the page, and a fourth voice model joins
the others. Mareader also gets a new icon. This release gathers
everything since v0.0.4.

## What's new

- **Read along.** The words light up as they're spoken, timed to the
  voice itself with Kokoro, Piper and Kitten. Light one word, a few, or
  a whole phrase; phrases break where the voice pauses.
- **A new player.** A compact player sits over the page. Drag its sides
  to make it wider or narrower. When the words being read scroll out of
  view, it grows into a card showing them; Settings › Read aloud chooses
  Full, Compact or the bar alone. It takes on the look of its book's
  pane, and its time left counts down evenly. When something goes wrong,
  it says what happened and how to fix it.
- **Start where you choose.** Right-click a word to read from it; the
  word lights up and the menu sits beside it, not over it. Select text
  to read only that. Right-click empty space to read from the start of
  that passage. A marker shows where reading will begin.
- **Reading carries on.** Speech is prepared about 20 seconds ahead, so
  sentences follow each other without a gap. Reading goes on after the
  book leaves its pane, and a book opened again picks up where it was
  being read.
- **Skip what reads badly aloud.** Tables, lists, figures and asides can
  each be read, skipped, or asked about every time, in EPUB, Markdown and
  text books.
- **Phonon, a fourth voice model.** English voices that read text as
  written, numbers, money and addresses included. It's a 64.5 MB
  download with 26 American and British voices of 256 KB each, and it
  reads at every speed from 0.5 to 3.
- **Kokoro Tiny.** A fourth Kokoro build of 92 MB, smaller than Small's
  141 MB, a little rougher in sound.
- **Voices start and speak sooner.** Models load in about half the time,
  and Kitten and Kokoro start speaking well over a second sooner.
- **A new dictionary.** The dictionary window and page are redesigned.
  The headword is spoken with your voice, or with another voice from the
  same model when yours doesn't speak that language. English IPA
  follows your voice's accent, American or British. Word cards show the
  word class and level after the headword.
- **Zoom that follows the pane.** Fitted zooms refit as the pane
  resizes. Turn on "Scale my zoom with the pane" in Settings › Layout,
  and a zoom you set shrinks with a narrower pane, then grows back, never
  past what you set.
- **A calmer library.** Covers look bound, reading progress shows as a
  bookmark ribbon, and organizing with AI gives quieter feedback.
- **A new icon:** an ivory open book with gilt edges and a gold ribbon
  on onyx.

## Fixes

- Wrapped lines in PDFs join into paragraphs, so they read aloud
  smoothly and reflow cleanly, and lines beside a figure wrap at it.
- A narrated book's highlight takes your accent colour, not the book's.
- Notifications move aside for menus and bars that open under them.
- Selected items stand out in light tinted themes.
- On macOS, the window buttons line up with the sidebar's icons at any
  interface zoom, and stay visible in light themes.
- Open… left the reader's menu; ⌘O or Ctrl+O still opens a book.

## Which file to download

| System | File | Install |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon | `Mareader_0.0.5_aarch64.dmg` | open it and drag Mareader to Applications |
| macOS 11 or later, Intel | `Mareader_0.0.5_x64.dmg` | the same |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `mareader_0.0.5_x64-setup.exe` | run it; it installs for you alone, no administrator needed |
| Windows, managed machines | `mareader_0.0.5_x64_en-US.msi` | `msiexec /i` it, or deploy it as usual |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS | `mareader_0.0.5_amd64.deb` | `sudo apt install ./mareader_0.0.5_amd64.deb` |
| Arch and its family | `mareader_0.0.5_x86_64.tar.gz` and `PKGBUILD` | put both in one folder, then `makepkg -si` |
| Any other Linux (glibc 2.35 or newer) | `mareader_0.0.5_x86_64.AppImage` | `chmod +x` it, then run it |

Installing over v0.0.4 keeps your library, settings and highlights. The
interface zoom goes back to 100% once. Piper now offers only voices
trained on public-domain recordings: Alba, Joe, Mike, Reza, Sam and
Kathleen are gone, and their files are deleted at start if you
downloaded them. Your system may keep showing the old icon until its
icon cache refreshes.
`SHA256SUMS` lists every file's checksum: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
--ignore-missing` on Linux, `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`
on a Mac.

## The first launch

This build is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so each system asks
once:

- **macOS:** "Apple could not verify Mareader". Choose Done, then open
  System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway next to
  Mareader. Later launches open directly.
- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run
  anyway.
- **Linux:** nothing to approve. The AppImage only needs to be executable.

## Known limits

- Neural voices are downloads of 24 MB to 327 MB each. The system voices
  need none. On Linux, the system voices go through Speech Dispatcher.
- Only Kokoro, Piper and Kitten say when each word is heard. With Phonon
  and the system voices the lit word follows the reading pace, so it can
  run a little ahead or behind.
- Skipping tables, lists, figures and asides works in EPUB, Markdown and
  text books, not PDFs.
- Your library stays on this computer; syncing between machines comes in
  a later release.
- Linux packages are for x86_64 only; ARM Linux and Windows on ARM have
  no build yet.

Before publishing, CI built every installer on its own system, then
installed each one, opened a book with it and removed it again.
