# G4 — the host, and the engine's own saving

Branch: `feat/engine-g4-host`, from `refactor/engine-g3-controllers` at `226ed54`
(CI quick **green** on `9799020`, run `43526824048`: 2047 tests, 0 failed). Review
phase: **P4**. One branch, committed in stages, as G3 was.

`ANALYSIS.md` §5 gives this slice its write-set — `engine/src/host.rs`,
`engine/src/save.rs`, `apps/mareader/src/main.rs`, `ui-egui/src/app/setup.rs` —
and its exit gate: *"`eframe::Storage` only in the importer + its fixture test;
interrupted-save test; `set_option` keeps UI-only keys."* Two findings of the
review are this slice's subject: **F6**, persistence through `eframe::Storage`,
and **F7**, `set_option` as a private string control channel. Two laws are its
measure: **Law 6**, the machine is a trait, and **Law 8**, the engine saves
itself. It also inherits the ruling G3 could not carry out: trapped ends at 7
there because the seven are P4's, and P4 deletes `tools/trapped_count.txt`.

G4 touches none of the three files `ANALYSIS.md` reserves — *"G0 and G6 are the
only slices that touch `AGENTS.md`, the parity ledger and `ROADMAP.md`. Nothing
else may."* Definition-of-done lines 8 and 9 (`mareader-cli commands`, the five
headless scripts) are G5's, and line 10 (every shipped parity entry citing
`command=` or `ui_only=`) is G6's; all three are reported at the foot of this
file rather than started here.

## The three rulings

Asked before any code was written, each with the measured alternatives:

| # | Question | Ruling (owner, 2026-11-13) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The engine owns saving, but wasm has no filesystem and the web app persists today through eframe's localStorage-backed `Storage`. How does the engine reach the bytes? | **The engine writes `settings.json` and `session.json` itself, from a `PathBuf` the host hands it.** `apps/mareader-web` keeps `eframe::Storage` for its two keys, so DoD #7 holds on desktop and is **reported unmet on web**. |
| 2 | `set_option`'s 94 keys: 57 already reach a `Command`, 32 touch only `self.ui`. What becomes of them? | **An engine parser.** Every key that sets engine state becomes `engine::options::parse(key, value) -> Result<Command, String>`, which is also what G5's `mareader-cli set` needs. Fixture keys become engine sample APIs. `set_option` keeps only what names the toolkit, the seven files stop being toolkit-free, trapped hits **0** and `tools/trapped_count.txt` is **deleted**. |
| 3 | `ANALYSIS.md` puts `engine/src/host.rs` in this write-set but its exit gate never mentions it, and Law 6 is not started. This slice or the next? | **The whole `Host` seam, in G4.** `trait Host` absorbs `Desk`'s 55 methods, and the eight `attach_*` methods plus the library's collapse into `Session::new(host, waker)`. |

Ruling 1 is the one that leaves a mark: the browser build keeps a second
persistence path, so `eframe::Storage` does not disappear from the repository —
it disappears from the engine's reach and from the front end's, and stays where
a browser's only store is. Ruling 3 is the one that costs the most: it touches
every file G3 settled, and `Session::execute(desk, intent, now)` becomes
`session.execute(intent, now)`.

## Part one — what is there to move, measured

### The persistence surface is two keys and five sites

`app.rs:76` and `:78` name them: `SETTINGS_KEY = "settings"`,
`LAST_DOCUMENT_KEY = "last_document"`. Everything else follows from those:

| Site | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `ui-egui/src/app/frame.rs:56` `fn save(&mut self, storage: &mut dyn Storage)` | Calls `self.ui.library.flush()`, writes `mareader_settings::to_json(self.settings())` under `"settings"`, and `serde_json::to_string(&self.last_document())` under `"last_document"` — `"null"` when nothing is open, so the next launch starts empty |
| `ui-egui/src/app/frame.rs:52` `fn auto_save_interval` | `SAVE_EVERY`, which is `Duration::from_millis(500)` at `app.rs:98` |
| `ui-egui/src/app.rs:254` `from_storage` | `settings_from(storage)`, then `LAST_DOCUMENT_KEY` parsed into `ui.last` |
| `ui-egui/src/app.rs:261` `settings_from` | `mareader_settings::load(&json)`, its repair notes to stderr, and a toast when any section was reset |
| `apps/mareader/src/main.rs:67` `fn save` | Forwards to the app's; `:63` forwards `auto_save_interval` |

Two test fakes implement `eframe::Storage for Memory` — `tests/reading.rs:477`
and `tests/shell.rs:474` — and three tests drive the pair directly:
`h.state_mut().save(&mut disk)` then `MareaderApp::from_storage(Some(&disk))`,
asserting on `disk.get_string(LAST_DOCUMENT_KEY)` (`reading.rs:670`, `:700`,
`:723`) and on `SETTINGS_KEY` (`shell.rs:506`, `:890`). Those become the
importer's fixture test, which is the second half of DoD #7's wording.

`eframe::App::save` has a default empty body, and so does `auto_save_interval`
(it defaults to 30 s), so deleting both on desktop is a deletion and not a
replacement: the engine writes when it decides to. `on_exit` is the hook that
replaces eframe's save-at-quit.

### The state directory already exists, and the engine already knows how to write

`apps/mareader/src/main.rs:124` calls `eframe::storage_dir("mareader")` and
joins `library`, `ai`, `dictionaries`, `vocabulary` and `voice-models` under it;
`cache_dir()` at `:166` derives a rebuildable-files dir from `LOCALAPPDATA`,
`HOME/Library/Caches` or `XDG_CACHE_HOME`. No crate names `data_dir`,
`config_dir` or `dirs::` — so the host supplies the path and the engine never
asks the platform. `eframe::storage_dir` returns a `PathBuf`, not a `Storage`,
which keeps it outside DoD #7's grep.

The write itself is `mareader_library::disk::write_atomic` at
`crates/library/src/disk.rs:328`: `create_dir_all` on the parent, a `.tmp`
sibling, `write_all`, `sync_all`, then `rename`, removing the temp on failure.
`mareader-library` is already an engine dependency, and `tempfile` is already an
engine dev-dependency, so the interrupted-save test needs no new manifest line.

The cadence has a house idiom to copy, in `engine/src/library/state.rs`:
`SAVE_DELAY: f64 = 0.5`, `save(&mut self, now) -> Option<f64>` returning the wait
left so the caller can schedule a frame, `model.take_dirty()`, and `flush()`
documented as *"Sends every pending change now: the app is saving or exiting."*
`crates/engine/Cargo.toml`'s own description already claims the result —
*"session, commands, events, jobs **and saving** (L5)"* — for a crate with no
`save.rs` in it.

None of the 94 reaches a toolkit call, which is exactly why the seven files are
trapped: they are toolkit-free files living in the front end. The review's own
description of the end state is that `set_option` *"keeps UI-only keys"* — window
size, hover point and platform skin — and a key that names an `egui::Vec2`, an
`egui::Pos2` or a `Platform` makes its file toolkit-bound, which is what frees
the count.

Two callers must keep working throughout: `examples/shot.rs:174-198`, which
takes `key=value` pairs on a shot's command line and also sets `voice_models`,
and `apps/mareader-web/src/lib.rs:37-40`, which feeds `?preset=sepia&panes=2`
through the same channel. `examples/memory.rs` is a third, using `find`,
`workers`, `panes`, `sidebar`, `library`, `layout`, `books`.

### `Desk` is 55 methods, and the machine is eight `attach_*` calls

`engine/src/desk.rs:44` declares `pub trait Desk` — *"The front end's
controllers, as the reducer calls them"* — with `session(&mut self) -> &mut
Session` first and 54 effect methods after it: `area`, `edge_tab`, `arrived`,
`close_pane`, `resume`, `open`, `zoom`, `set_mode`, `arrow`, `page_key`,
`toggle_drift`, `go_to`, `turn`, `copy_pages`, `save` (an export, not
persistence), `book`, `path`, `restart_at_start`, `set_appearance`,
`open_settings`, `word_list`, `word_window`, `study`, `explain`, `look_up`,
`highlight`, `note`, `bookmark`, `select`, `dict_window`, `pursue`, `dict`,
`vocab`, `pronunciation`, `page_ai`, `ai`, `place_read_marker`,
`dismiss_read_markers`, `read_aloud`, `toggle_read_aloud`, `stop_read_aloud`,
`try_voice`, `try_voice_of`, `stop_voice_try`, `say_word`, `stop_saying`,
`reveal_voice`, `voice_model`, `open_book`, `library`. Two types answer it:
`Front<'_>` at `ui-egui/src/app/apply/desk.rs:31` (283 lines) and `Headless` at
`engine/tests/common/mod.rs:75` (290 lines, constructed by `Headless::new(out)`).

The reducer is `Session::execute(desk: &mut dyn Desk, intent: Command, now: f64)`
at `engine/src/execute.rs:20` — a static method that borrows the session back out
of the desk (`desk.session().route()`, `.apply()`, `.emit()`). `Session::new`
takes only `Settings` (`session.rs:62`) and `set_waker` is separate (`:77`).

The machine arrives after construction, in `ui-egui/src/app/setup.rs`:
`attach_library(ctx, root)`, `attach_ai(ctx, root, helper)`,
`attach_dictionaries(ctx, dir)`, `attach_vocabulary(ctx, dir)`,
`attach_voice_models(ctx, dir)`, `attach_voice_models_from(ctx, dir, catalogue)`,
`attach_voices(dir, catalogue, waker)` and `attach_cache(dir)` — eight methods,
six of them `#[cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))]`, each cloning a `Context` into
a `Waker` and pushing a toast when a service refuses to start. `resume()` in the
same file turns `ui.last` into an `OpenRequest`, and is the reader of the second
persisted key.

`MareaderApp` is `{ session, ui }`, so a `Host` held by the session cannot also
lend it back: `Host` takes the 54 effect methods and the machine's, `Desk`'s
`session()` goes, and `execute` becomes a method on `Session`.

## Part two — the plan

A stage is one closed set and one commit. CI is watched once, at the end of the
slice, not at every stage: the owner's ruling, repeated for this slice.

Mode was `ci` while the first four stages were written, and is `cache` now. Two
things changed that, both recorded under *What landed*: `Check` keeps its build
when a run is red, so this branch has a cache of its own at last, and the sandbox
cache G3's tip packed is restored here, which puts `cargo fmt`, a scoped
`cargo clippy --all-targets -D warnings` and `cargo xtask` in the sandbox at the
price of about ten seconds. The Python gates still run on every stage:
`shape_check.py --dups`, `comment_check.py`, `comment_budget.py` and
`commit_check.py`. Tests are still CI's, because the cache holds lint builds
only.

### Stage 1 — `engine/src/save.rs`, with the interrupted-save test

The engine's own store, and nothing calls it yet.

- `save::Save { dir: Option<PathBuf>, wrote: f64, dirty: bool }`, on `Session` as
  a field, with `save(&mut self, now) -> Option<f64>` and `flush(&mut self)`
  copying `library/state.rs`'s idiom and its `SAVE_DELAY` of half a second.
- Two files under `dir`: `settings.json` (`mareader_settings::to_json`) and
  `session.json` (the last document, `null` when nothing is open). Both through
  `disk::write_atomic`. `dir: None` — the browser, a test — writes nothing.
- `save::load(dir) -> Loaded { settings, last, notes }`, reusing
  `mareader_settings::load` so unreadable settings still repair section by
  section, with the notes for the caller to toast.
- `save_tests.rs`, exempt from shape as `memory_tests.rs` and `search_tests.rs`
  are, over a `tempfile::tempdir()`: a good round trip; a `.tmp` sibling left by
  an interrupted write is ignored and the file beside it still loads; a truncated
  `settings.json` loads defaults and reports a note; a `session.json` that will
  not parse leaves `last` empty rather than failing the launch; `dir: None` reads
  and writes nothing.

**Gate:** the engine's tests, and `Save`'s two files written by nobody but them.

### Stage 2 — the importer, which is DoD #7

- `ui-egui/src/import.rs`: reads `eframe::Storage` **once**, at creation. If the
  engine's own files are absent and Storage holds the old keys, it migrates them
  — writes both through stage 1 — and returns what it loaded. This is the only
  `eframe::Storage` in the front end's non-test code, and the fixture test beside
  it is the other place the gate allows.
- `MareaderApp::from_storage` and `settings_from` go; `from_creation` takes the
  dir the app hands it. `fn save` and `fn auto_save_interval` are deleted from
  `app/frame.rs` on desktop, and `apps/mareader/src/main.rs` stops forwarding
  them, gaining an `on_exit` that flushes.
- The browser keeps its store, per ruling 1: a `Web { app }` wrapper in
  `apps/mareader-web/src/lib.rs` holds the wasm-only `save` and
  `auto_save_interval`, forwarding `clear_color`, `logic` and `ui`. That file is
  not in `ANALYSIS.md`'s write-set for this slice and is reported as an addition.
- `library.flush()` moves inside the engine's own save, so the library's pending
  rows and the two files are written on one cadence.
- The three tests that drive `save`/`from_storage` move to the importer's fixture
  test; `tests/reading.rs`'s and `tests/shell.rs`'s `Memory` fakes go with them.

**Gate:** `rg 'eframe::Storage' crates apps` prints the importer, its fixture
test and the web wrapper — and nothing in `crates/engine`.

### Stage 3 — the cadence

`SAVE_EVERY` at `app.rs:98` and the two `request_repaint_after(SAVE_EVERY +
50ms)` hooks at `app/frame/input.rs:30` and `app/frame/chrome.rs:238` become the
engine's `save(now) -> Option<f64>`: the frame asks the session whether a write
is due, and schedules the frame it is told to. `Done.dirty` (`session.rs:43`) is
a redraw flag, not a persistence flag, so `Save.dirty` is set where persisted
state actually changes — settings through `put`, the last document through the
routes that move a pane.

### Stage 4 — `engine/src/host.rs`, which is Law 6

The largest stage, and the one that touches G3's settled files.

- `pub trait Host`: `Desk`'s 54 effect methods, plus the machine's —
  `state_dir()`, `cache_dir()`, `library_root()`, `dictionaries_dir()`,
  `vocabulary_dir()`, `voice_models_dir()`, `ai_root()`, and the wakers the eight
  `attach_*` methods build from a `Context` today.
- `Session { …, host: Box<dyn Host> }` and `Session::new(host, waker)`; settings
  arrive through the host, so the importer's result is an argument to it rather
  than a field assigned after.
- `Session::execute(&mut self, intent, now) -> Done` replaces
  `execute(desk, intent, now)`; `desk.session()` calls become `self`.
- `impl Desk for Front<'_>` becomes `impl Host for Front<'_>`, and `Headless`
  answers the machine's methods with its temp dir — which is what makes a
  headless client possible, and is G5's foundation.
- The eight `attach_*` collapse: the dirs are read from the host when the session
  starts, and the toasts a refused service produces come back through `drain()`.

**Gate:** `rg 'trait Desk' crates` empty, `Session::new` taking two arguments,
and the engine's own tests constructing a session with no front end at all.

### Stages 5 to 10 — `set_option`'s 94 keys, by family

`engine::options::parse(key, value) -> Result<Command, String>`, one stage per
family, each leaving the ui handler a one-line forward until the family is empty:
appearance (38 keys), library (17), panes (15), study (15), read_aloud (7),
page_ai (2). Fixture keys —
`books`, `shelves`, `copies`, `runs`, `dictionaries`, `voice_models`, `workers`
— become engine sample APIs beside `sample::books`, since a fixture that fills a
library is engine state and not a view. Keys that name a toolkit type stay up:
window size, hover point, platform skin, and whatever `chromeless`, `menu`,
`sidebar` and `find` turn out to need once the rest have gone.

`examples/shot.rs`, `examples/memory.rs` and the web app's query string keep
working at every stage, because `set_option` remains the entry point and only its
body changes.

### The last stage — trapped 0, and the gate becomes absolute

When the seven files hold only toolkit-bound keys they stop being toolkit-free,
`xtask trapped` finds nothing beyond `ALLOWED`, and `tools/trapped_count.txt` is
deleted. `xtask/src/trapped.rs:101` currently fails on a missing count file —
*"`{COUNT_FILE}` is missing; commit the count it gates"* — so `committed()` and
`run()` go with it and the gate becomes `left.is_empty()`, with its three tests
rewritten to match. That is the review's P3 exit line, paid one phase late, and
the ratchet never comes back.

### Then the docs

`docs/memory.md` for what is written where and when, `docs/architecture.md` for
Law 8 met and Law 6's trait, and `docs/slices/G4.md`'s own ledger. `AGENTS.md`,
`parity/mareader-features.toml` and `ROADMAP.md` stay untouched, as reserved.

**The branch had no cache, and every push paid for it.** Run `43551162550`
restored `v0-rust-linux-Linux-x64-c893db98` and found nothing, so each push
compiled about 300 dependencies before it reached the first of our own crates:
14 to 21 minutes, and a red run's build thrown away, because `rust-cache` leaves
`CACHE_ON_FAILURE` off and every run on this branch had been red. G3's caches
belong to a branch this one cannot restore from, and main sits 157 commits back
at `bf2cd85` with its own `Check` cache long evicted. `cache-on-failure: true`
on `Check` is the fix: the dependencies are the expensive part, the workspace's
own crates are not cached at all, so a failure in them poisons nothing. The
other three `rust-cache` steps still save on main only.

**The sandbox cache is here, and it changed how a stage is checked.** G3's tip
packed one at `9799020`, six commits behind this head; `tools/ci.py cache
restore` fetched its 1810 MB and unpacked it under `/home/user/.cache`, which
needs `GITHUB_TOKEN` — the sandbox has a PAT and `ci.py` does not look for the
name it is kept under, so `mode` said `cache` was out until it was exported.
`sudo apt-get install libclang-dev libasound2-dev libspeechd-dev` was needed for
`speech-dispatcher-sys`'s build script, and apt is not part of a snapshot, so a
wiped sandbox installs it again. Since then a stage is fmt-checked, clippy-clean
over `mareader-engine`, `mareader-ui-egui` and `mareader` with `--all-targets
-D warnings`, and run past `cargo xtask layers`, `trapped`, `unsafe` and `assets`

run on: a `let...else` clippy reads as `?`, a trait alias needs `dyn` and the
lifetime of the reference it is used through, `Machine` holds `PathBuf` and so
is not `Copy`, and `import/tests.rs` named no toolkit, which put `trapped` at
eight against a ceiling of seven until the fixture's impl said
`eframe::Storage`.

**Stage 2 and stage 3 landed as one commit**, because deleting `frame.rs`'s
`save` and adding the frame's `keep` are the same change seen from two sides:
either both are in a commit or the desktop build has no writer at all.

**The browser's store is not where the plan put it.** Stage 2 wanted a
`Web { app }` wrapper in `apps/mareader-web/src/lib.rs` holding the wasm-only
`save` and `auto_save_interval`. Those two methods are in the front end's own
`impl eframe::App for MareaderApp` instead, behind `cfg(target_arch = "wasm32")`,
forwarding to `import::keep_browser`. The web app is untouched, so the slice
stays inside the write-set `ANALYSIS.md` gives it, and the one name of
`eframe::Storage` in the front end is the importer's alias `Store`.

**`Host` did not take the machine's methods.** The plan had `state_dir()`,
`cache_dir()` and five more on the trait, and `Session::new(host, waker)` holding
it. A directory is data, not an effect: `Front<'a>` borrows the app for the
length of one command, so a session cannot own the thing that borrows it back,
and eight trait methods the front end would answer from `session.machine()` are
indirection with nothing on the other side. `Machine` is a struct in `host.rs`,
the session holds it, and `Session::new` keeps its one argument — the machine
arrives through `set_machine`, as the waker and the save directory already do.
`execute` stays `Session::execute(host, intent, now)` for the same reason.

**`library.flush()` is still the front end's call.** `MareaderApp::flush` writes
the library's rows and then settles the session's two files, on one cadence and
in one place, but the rows belong to `LibraryState` in the front end's state and
`Save` would have to own it to write them.

## Reported, not done

- **DoD #7 does not hold on web.** Ruling 1 leaves the browser writing its store,
  because a browser's only store is the browser's. The name lives in
  `ui-egui/src/import.rs` and its fixture test, and the wasm-only half of the
  front end's `eframe::App` impl reaches it through the importer's alias; the
  engine has none of it and `apps/mareader-web` is untouched. The line is met for
  the engine and the front end on desktop, and the exception is named here rather
  than hidden behind a `cfg`.
- **Stages 5 to 10 are not started**, and with them the third line of the exit
  gate: `set_option` still owns all 94 keys across the seven trapped files,
  `engine::options::parse` does not exist, `tools/trapped_count.txt` is still
  committed and the gate still reads a ceiling of 7 rather than an empty list.
  The first two lines are met: `eframe::Storage` is in the importer and its
  fixture test alone, and the interrupted-save test is `save_tests.rs`'s
  `a_temp_file_left_by_an_interrupted_write_is_not_read`.
- **DoD #8 and #9 are G5's.** `apps/mareader-cli` does not exist, so nothing
  prints a spec per `Command` variant and no headless script runs in CI. Stage 4's
  `Headless` answering `Host` is the groundwork, and `SPECS`, `Command::id()` and
  `tests/specs.rs` are already exact — 60 ids, 60 arms, no duplicates.
- **DoD #10 is G6's**, and cannot be started here even if it were wanted:
  `xtask/src/parity.rs:35` carries `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]`, so writing
  `command=` or `ui_only=` into the ledger breaks `cargo xtask parity` until the
  struct and its validation change — the ledger and the tool being G6's write-set.
- **Exports still write through the front end.** `Desk::save(name, format,
  contents)` lands in `ui-egui/src/files.rs:139`, whose three dialog threads wrap
  `rfd` in `pollster::block_on`. Law 6's dialog-as-`UiCommand` work takes those,
  and G3's owner ruling already accepted `files.rs` as the fifth thread-spawning
  file until it does.
- **`persist_window: true`** at `apps/mareader/src/main.rs:136` leaves window
  geometry in eframe's own `egui_storage.json`. That is the viewport's, not the
  app's state, and no code of this repository reads it.
