### Why `Repaint` exists

Law 5 sends a wake out through `Waker = Arc<dyn Fn() + Send + Sync>`, which is
all a worker that *finishes* needs. A controller that polls an engine only
answering when asked, or waits for a settle time to arrive, has to say **when**,
and a closure taking nothing cannot. So those wait in the view, and that is the
only thing pinning three files to a `Context`:

| File | Line | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_aloud/booth.rs` | 177 | `ctx.request_repaint_after(TICK)` — some systems only report a finished sample when polled |
| `read_aloud/spoken.rs` | 658, 676, 695 | the same `TICK` poll, three places |
| `library/folders.rs` | 263 | `request_repaint_after(at - now)` — wake when a rescan's settle time arrives |
| `page_ai/follow.rs` | 297, 313, 327 | a state machine's dwell and retry waits |

`Repaint` sits **beside** `Waker` rather than replacing it: the review fixes that
alias, and `speech`, `assist`, `dict` and `cefr` all export it, so it cannot take
an argument. One closure taking a `Duration`, zero for at once.

## Part two — the three facts that dictate the plan

These were measured, and each one rules out an approach that looks obvious.

### Fact 1 — trapped has zero headroom, so nothing may be freed in place

`tools/trapped_count.txt` is **10** and the count is **10**. The gate fails when
`found > ceiling`, so:

> **Any commit that makes a `ui-egui` file toolkit-free without moving it into
> `crates/engine` in the same commit turns CI red.**

That rules out the natural incremental plan — "first decouple every controller
from `Context`, then move the freed files down". Concretely: converting
`spoken.rs` to `Repaint` removes all six of its toolkit lines, and the commit
that does it must also move the file, or trapped goes 10 → 11.

It also means P3 cannot go one file at a time where files import each other.
Each stage has to move a **closed set**.

### Fact 2 — the coupling is tiny; the interleaving is the problem

Toolkit lines against file size, for the review's in-scope files:

```
  7 toolkit lines / 1309 total   study/mod.rs
  6 toolkit lines / 1106 total   read_aloud/spoken.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  457 total   page_ai/groups.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  399 total   word_list/mod.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  408 total   library/io.rs
 15 toolkit lines / 1727 total   documents.rs
 56 toolkit lines / 1933 total   read_aloud/mod.rs
```

Decoupling is hours of mechanical work. What is hard is that the engine half is
*interleaved* with the view half, so a file cannot be cut cleanly:

- `spoken.rs` imports `super::card::Lyrics` (13 toolkit words) and
  `super::control::{Act, View}` (36), and `ReadAloud` holds a
  `Session::Spoken(Box<Spoken>)` variant — so `Spoken` is pinned by the card's
  lyrics and the control's view.
- `page_ai` shares `Pill`/`Ring` (`paint.rs`) and `Around`/`Asked` across all six
  of its files.
- the trapped `library/{actions,organizing}.rs` import `super::LibraryState` and
  `crate::ui_command::{Commands, Sent, UiCommand}`.

This is the same wall G2 recorded for `PdfView`: *"59 match sites rewriting what
a `PdfView` is, and with no compiler that is a change I could not verify."*

### Fact 3 — `Desk` says exactly what is left, and who owns it

`crates/engine/src/desk.rs` is documented as *"the list of what P3 has left to
move"*. Its 51 methods, grouped by the controller the adapter reaches
(a `*` means the adapter still hands it a `Context`):

| Controller | Desk methods | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **documents** | **16** | `open*`, `zoom`, `set_mode`, `arrow`, `page_key`, `toggle_drift`, `go_to`, `turn`, `book`, `path`, `restart_at_start*`, `select`, `place_read_marker`, `dismiss_read_markers`, plus `close_pane` and `copy_pages` shared with `page_ai`/`page_copy` |
| **read_aloud** | 7 | `toggle_read_aloud`, `stop_read_aloud`, `try_voice`, `try_voice_of*`, `stop_voice_try`, `stop_saying`, `voice_model` |
| session / chrome | 19 | already the session's, or a `UiCommand`'s |
| dict, library, files, word_lists, word_window, vocab, sounds, ai | 1–2 each | |

16 of the 51 still take a `Context` through the adapter.

## The plan

Ordered so that every stage is a closed set: nothing is freed without being
moved, and no stage depends on a later one. Each stage ends with the Python
gates green here, one push, and one CI verdict.

### Stage 4 — `Booth` and `tone` cross (the one genuinely free piece)

`booth.rs` is 219 lines with **four** toolkit lines, and all four are a
`Context`: building a `Waker` in `start`, threading `ctx` through `open` and
`try_voice_of`, and the `TICK` poll in `tick`. After `Repaint` it names no
toolkit at all.

Its only two upward dependencies are small and both can cross with it:

- `super::TICK` — `const TICK: Duration = Duration::from_millis(50)`, in
  `read_aloud/mod.rs:39`.
- `super::spoken::tone` — five lines, `ReadAloudSettings` → `mareader_speech::Tone`,
  no toolkit, no view type.

**Write-set:** new `crates/engine/src/read_aloud/booth.rs` (the `Booth` struct,
`TICK`, `tone`); `crates/engine/src/read_aloud/mod.rs` gains the module;
`ui-egui/src/read_aloud/booth.rs` deleted; `ui-egui/src/read_aloud/mod.rs` loses
`TICK` and `tone` and re-points `mod booth`/`pub use`; callers at
`app/read_aloud.rs:17-18`, `app/apply/desk.rs:243`, `read_aloud/mod.rs:830,926,1003`
build a `Repaint` with `crate::repaint::of(ctx)` instead of passing `ctx`.

**Two hazards, both found by reading:**

- `Booth::close()` does `*self = Self::default()` and then restores `said`. A
  held `Repaint` must be preserved the same way, or closing a booth silently
  drops its repaints and the next sample never lands.
- `mareader_speech::open` needs a `Waker`, not a `Repaint`. Build one from the
  `Repaint` — `Arc::new(move || repaint.now())` — rather than keeping a `Context`.

**Gate effect:** trapped unchanged at 10 (`booth.rs` was toolkit-bound, so it was
never counted). Thread files unchanged. What it buys is the first whole job owner
below the line, and the pattern for the rest.

### Stage 5 — `spoken.rs`: the engine half down, `Lyrics` and `View` up

`Spoken` is 1,106 lines, six toolkit lines, one thread. It cannot move whole
(Fact 2), so it splits: the sentence-making thread, the read-ahead window
(`AHEAD`/`BEHIND`/`LOOK`/`LOOK_CHARS`/`LOOK_MOST`) and the `Spot` arithmetic go
down; `Lyrics` and `control::{Act, View}` stay, reached the way `Desk` reaches a
controller today.

This is the stage that first uses `Repaint` for a real tick: lines 658, 676 and
695 become `repaint.after(TICK)`.

**Gate effect:** one thread file removed from the front end (**15 → 14**), and
trapped must not move — the engine half leaves, the view half keeps naming
egui, so `spoken.rs` stays toolkit-bound and uncounted.

### Stage 6 — the `page_ai` family (six files, 4,235 lines)

The largest single win: three thread files (`groups.rs`, `follow.rs`, `cards.rs`)
and eight of the fourteen `ctx.input(|i| i.time)` clock reads.

- `groups.rs` (457, four toolkit lines) needs only `now: f64` and a `Waker`.
- `follow.rs` (508, seven) needs those plus `Repaint` for its three waits.
- `mod.rs` (1,687, thirteen) holds `PageAi`'s state: `batches`, `runs`, `showing`.
- `menu.rs` (804) is view — pointer and key input, `ctx.input_mut`.
- **`cards.rs` cannot move.** Lines 212-214 build an `egui::ColorImage` and call
  `ctx.load_texture`; limit 5 ("no pixels below L6") keeps that up. It splits.
- `paint.rs` (452) is view by definition.

`engine::page_ai` already exists (`mod.rs` 71 lines of types, `extract.rs` 339),
so the engine half has a home.

**Gate effect:** thread files **14 → 11**; trapped unchanged unless
`library`/`trigger` move here.

### Stage 7 — the `LibraryState` cluster

`library.rs` (535), `io.rs` (408, one thread), `folders.rs` (726), `runs.rs`
(396), and the trapped `actions.rs` (119) and `organizing.rs` (716).
`engine::library` already holds the model half (`state.rs`, `books.rs`, `find.rs`,
`moving.rs`, `shelving.rs`, `held.rs`, `question.rs`, `duplicating.rs`,
`counted.rs` — 1,634 lines), so this is the mirror of what G2's stage 14 did.

The review states the order: *"`LibraryState` has to cross before any of its
`impl` blocks can."* So `LibraryState` itself moves, and `actions.rs` and
`organizing.rs` come with it.

`folders.rs` also needs `ctx.input(|i| (i.time, i.focused))` split: `now` becomes
an argument, but **window focus stays a view concern** — the rescan-on-regain-
focus rule reads it, so that half does not move.

**Gate effect:** thread files **11 → 10**; trapped **10 → 8**.

### Stage 8 — `study`, `word_list`, `search`, `sounds`, `vocab`, `dict`, `ai`

- `study/mod.rs` (1,309, seven toolkit lines) **splits, it does not move**: line
  625 reads `ctx.pointer_latest_pos()`, 1076 reads `ctx.input(|i| …)`, 1145

- `word_list/mod.rs` (399, four): three are a `Waker`; `ctx.copy_text` at 316

- `search.rs` (382, ten), `sounds.rs` (632, five — its lookup worker is already

- `vocab.rs` (329) and `dict/mod.rs` (551) are **mostly view**: `ui.data`,

### Stage 9 — `trigger.rs` and the last of trapped

`trigger.rs` (201) is trapped by `crate::read_aloud::ReadAloud` and

**Gate effect:** thread files **5 → 4** (the allowlist); trapped **8 → 7**.

## The two places P3's exit gate cannot be met as written

Both are reported rather than worked around, as G2's stage 13 did.

### 1. trapped reaches 7, not 0, because 7 of them are P4's

The trapped 10 are:

| Files | Lines | What moves them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `app/options.rs` + its six children | 1,187 | **P4 item 5**, which replaces every `set_option` key that sets engine state with a command id and leaves only window size, hover point and platform skin |
| `library/actions.rs`, `library/organizing.rs` | 835 | stage 7 |
| `trigger.rs` | 201 | stage 9 |

So P3 can take trapped from 10 to **7**, and the remaining seven are the headless
knobs `examples/shot.rs` and the tests drive. The review's own P1 section says a
held file *"moves in the phase that moves its last egui-bound import"*, and these
move in P4. Deleting `tools/trapped_count.txt` at the end of P3 would leave the
gate failing on seven files P3 has no mandate to touch.

**Two honest ways out, owner's choice:**

- **(a)** P3 lowers the ceiling to 7 and leaves the count file; P4 deletes it.
  The ratchet keeps working and no rule is bent.
- **(b)** Once P4 item 5 has left `app/options*.rs` holding only window size,
  hover point and platform skin, allowlist them as `dial_deck/labels.rs` was —
  `ALLOWED` has no size limit, and they would by then be genuine front-end state.
  That cannot happen inside P3, because today they still set engine state.

(a) is recommended: it needs no judgement about what `set_option` will look like
after P4, and it keeps the count falling under the same rule all the way down.

### 2. "Every job the app can start has an engine test" is a large surface

The review asks for a test per job that starts it, waits on its `Waker` and reads
its `Event`. Today the engine has `tests/replay.rs` (open, split, turn, mark,
export) and `tests/specs.rs` (all 60 variants have a row), plus the four new
`repaint` tests. The jobs P3 moves — page-copy (done), the page-AI groups, the
spoken-word thread, the library walk, search, word lists, the booth's sample —
each need one over a windowless `Desk`, which `tests/common/mod.rs` already
provides.

That is roughly seven new harnesses. They are the point of the phase, not
bookkeeping: an engine test that starts a job is the only proof the job survives
without a window.

## Verification, and its cost

Mode is **`ci`** on the owner's instruction: nothing compiles here. `ci.py mode`
recommends `cache` and the sandbox cache is warm and current for this branch, but
the owner's Rule 1 overrides detection, so no `cargo` runs locally.

What runs here, and has never disagreed with CI: `shape_check.py --dups`,
`comment_check.py`, `comment_budget.py`, `commit_check.py`, and hand-rolled
replicas of `trapped` and the engine toolkit grep.

What only CI can see: unused imports, derives, borrows, anything under
`-D warnings`. Two red runs so far were exactly that — `#[derive(Debug)]` on a
struct holding a `Waker` (`dyn Fn()` is not `Debug`), and an unused `FULL_TINT`.

**The discipline that keeps the cache alive:** push once, wait for the CI run to
*complete*, wait for `Cache / sandbox` to *complete*, and only then push again.
`concurrency: ci-${ref}-${event_name}` with `cancel-in-progress: true` means a
new push cancels the in-flight one, and `refresh` only fires `needs: check` on
success — so pushing early cancels the job that builds the cache. Cold ≈ 17 min,
warm ≈ 5½ min.

Stage sizes are chosen to fit that loop: a stage is one closed set, one push, one
verdict. Nothing is batched across stages, because a red run then names two
stages' worth of failures at once.
