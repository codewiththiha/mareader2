### Why `Repaint` exists

Law 5 sends a wake out through `Waker = Arc<dyn Fn() + Send + Sync>`, which is
all a worker that *finishes* needs. A controller that polls an engine only
answering when asked, or waits for a settle time to arrive, has to say **when**,
and a closure taking nothing cannot. So those wait in the view, and that is the
only thing pinning three files to a `Context`:

| File | Line | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_aloud/booth.rs` | 177 | `ctx.request_repaint_after(TICK)` — some systems only report a finished sample when polled |
| `read_aloud/spoken.rs` | 658, 676, 695 | the same `TICK` poll, three places |
| `library/folders.rs` | 263 | `request_repaint_after(at - now)` — wake when a rescan's settle time arrives |
| `page_ai/follow.rs` | 297, 313, 327 | a state machine's dwell and retry waits |

`Repaint` sits **beside** `Waker` rather than replacing it: the review fixes that
alias, and `speech`, `assist`, `dict` and `cefr` all export it, so it cannot take
an argument. One closure taking a `Duration`, zero for at once.

## Part two — the three facts that dictate the plan

These were measured, and each one rules out an approach that looks obvious.

### Fact 1 — trapped has zero headroom, so nothing may be freed in place

`tools/trapped_count.txt` is **10** and the count is **10**. The gate fails when
`found > ceiling`, so:

> **Any commit that makes a `ui-egui` file toolkit-free without moving it into
> `crates/engine` in the same commit turns CI red.**

That rules out the natural incremental plan — "first decouple every controller
from `Context`, then move the freed files down". Concretely: converting
`spoken.rs` to `Repaint` removes all six of its toolkit lines, and the commit
that does it must also move the file, or trapped goes 10 → 11.

It also means P3 cannot go one file at a time where files import each other.
Each stage has to move a **closed set**.

### Fact 2 — the coupling is tiny; the interleaving is the problem

Toolkit lines against file size, for the review's in-scope files:

```
  7 toolkit lines / 1309 total   study/mod.rs
  6 toolkit lines / 1106 total   read_aloud/spoken.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  457 total   page_ai/groups.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  399 total   word_list/mod.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  408 total   library/io.rs
 15 toolkit lines / 1727 total   documents.rs
 56 toolkit lines / 1933 total   read_aloud/mod.rs
```

Decoupling is hours of mechanical work. What is hard is that the engine half is
*interleaved* with the view half, so a file cannot be cut cleanly:

- `spoken.rs` imports `super::card::Lyrics` (13 toolkit words) and
  `super::control::{Act, View}` (36), and `ReadAloud` holds a
  `Session::Spoken(Box<Spoken>)` variant — so `Spoken` is pinned by the card's
  lyrics and the control's view.
- `page_ai` shares `Pill`/`Ring` (`paint.rs`) and `Around`/`Asked` across all six
  of its files.
- the trapped `library/{actions,organizing}.rs` import `super::LibraryState` and
  `crate::ui_command::{Commands, Sent, UiCommand}`.

This is the same wall G2 recorded for `PdfView`: *"59 match sites rewriting what
a `PdfView` is, and with no compiler that is a change I could not verify."*

### Fact 3 — `Desk` says exactly what is left, and who owns it

`crates/engine/src/desk.rs` is documented as *"the list of what P3 has left to
move"*. Its 51 methods, grouped by the controller the adapter reaches
(a `*` means the adapter still hands it a `Context`):

| Controller | Desk methods | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **documents** | **16** | `open*`, `zoom`, `set_mode`, `arrow`, `page_key`, `toggle_drift`, `go_to`, `turn`, `book`, `path`, `restart_at_start*`, `select`, `place_read_marker`, `dismiss_read_markers`, plus `close_pane` and `copy_pages` shared with `page_ai`/`page_copy` |

## The plan

Ordered so that every stage is a closed set: nothing is freed without being
moved, and no stage depends on a later one. Each stage ends with the Python
gates green here, one push, and one CI verdict.

### Stage 4 — `Booth`, `TICK` and `tone` cross (the one genuinely free piece)

`booth.rs` was 219 lines with **four** toolkit lines, and all four were a
`Context`: building a `Waker` in `start`, threading `ctx` through `open` and
`try_voice_of`, and the `TICK` poll in `tick`. After `Repaint` it names no
toolkit at all.

Its only two upward dependencies were small and both crossed with it:

- `super::TICK` — `const TICK: Duration = Duration::from_millis(50)`, in
  `read_aloud/mod.rs:39`.
- `super::spoken::tone` — five lines, `ReadAloudSettings` → `mareader_speech::Tone`,
  no toolkit, no view type. It joins `TICK` in `engine::read_aloud`'s root,
  beside `ReadFrom`, because both `Booth` and `spoken.rs` want it.

**What the write-set turned out to be.** New `crates/engine/src/read_aloud/booth.rs`
(357 lines with its tests); `TICK` and `tone` into `engine/src/read_aloud/mod.rs`;
`ui-egui/src/read_aloud/booth.rs` deleted; `ui-egui/src/read_aloud/mod.rs`
re-points `pub use Booth` at the engine and gains `attach_repaint`; `spoken.rs`
names the engine's `TICK` and `tone` where it used to name its own; and the four
callers — `app/read_aloud.rs`, `app/frame/chrome.rs`, `app/glosses.rs`,
`app/apply/desk.rs` — pass one argument fewer.

**The shape that mattered: attach once, not per call.** The obvious edit is
`ctx` → `&Repaint` in each signature. But `voices_shown` runs *every frame* while
a voice tab shows, so `repaint::of(ctx)` at the call site would allocate an `Arc`
per frame for a booth that is usually already open. So `Booth` holds its
`Repaint` and `attach`es it once, in `app/frame.rs`'s first-frame block beside
`page_copy.attach` and `session.set_waker` — the house pattern `dict.attach`,
`vocab.attach` and `sounds.attach` already use. `open`, `try_voice_of` and `tick`
then lose `ctx` outright rather than swapping it for another argument, and
`Desk::try_voice_of` stops borrowing the adapter's `Context`: 16 of its methods
did, now 15.

**Both hazards the plan named were real.**

- `close()` does `*self = Self::default()` and restores `said`. With attach-once
  the `Repaint` has to be restored beside it, or closing a booth — which happens
  whenever a voice tab hides, and whenever a model is removed — would drop the
  window handle for good and the booth would never ask for a frame again.
  `a_close_lets_the_engine_go_but_keeps_the_window` reaches into the private
  field to prove it survives.
- `mareader_speech::open` wants a `Waker`, not a `Repaint`, so `start` builds one
  from the held copy: `Arc::new(move || repaint.now())`. No `Context` is kept.

**Seven engine tests**, over `mareader_speech::SILENT` — the mute engine
`crates/speech/src/silent.rs` documents as being "for tests and shots", whose
`events()` reports `Started` on the first look and `Finished` on the second. That
makes the seam observable without an audio backend: a sample waiting to end asks
for exactly one frame after `TICK`, and asks for nothing once the end is heard.
This is the first job in P3 with the engine test the exit gate asks for.

**Two constraints worth recording.**

- `read_aloud/mod.rs` has **zero line slack** (1933 of a 1933 ceiling), as does
  `spoken.rs` (1106). `attach_repaint` cost five lines, so five had to come back:
  `mod booth`, the local `TICK` and its doc, and a stray
  `use mareader_engine::read_aloud::ReadFrom` sitting mid-file at line 77 with a
  blank line either side, folded into the import block at the top. `spoken.rs`
  gave back eight by losing `tone`, and names the engine's `TICK` and `tone` in
  full at its five call sites rather than spending a line on an import it has no
  room for. `attach_repaint` is the one new public method in the file with no doc
  comment, because the sixth line did not exist; `pub fn active` and
  `pub fn stop_word` beside it are the precedent.
- `pub use booth::Booth` stayed, repointed at the engine, so
  `crate::read_aloud::Booth` still resolves for `settings_window/read_aloud.rs`
  (694 of 694 — also zero slack) and `voice_model.rs`. Limit 3 allows an
  import-path edit in the consumer instead, but rustfmt's ordering of a `use`
  block is not something to guess at with no formatter to hand.

**Gate effect:** trapped unchanged at 10, as predicted — `booth.rs` named a
toolkit, so it was never counted, and the file that replaces it is in the engine.
Thread files unchanged at 15: `Booth` spawns nothing. `spoken.rs` shrank
1106 → 1098 but the ceiling was left alone, because the repo re-records ceilings
in their own commit (`76095fa build(shape): re-record the ceilings`) rather than
inside a refactor.

### Stage 5 — the reading thread down; `Spoken` itself stays (landed)

The plan said `Spoken` would split, its engine half going down and `Lyrics` and
`control::{Act, View}` staying up. Reading the file showed a better cut was
available: **`Spoken` did not need to split at all.** Its last 139 lines were
already a self-contained tail — `Reach`, `enough`, `spawn`, `read`, `anchor`,
`locate` and their test — naming no toolkit and no view type, and the seven
definitions it needs from the head (`Start`, `Said`, `Counts`, `AHEAD`, `LOOK`,
`LOOK_CHARS`, `LOOK_MOST`) are all data or arithmetic.

So the tail moved whole to `engine::read_aloud::feed` (241 lines with three
tests) and `Spoken` stayed exactly as it was, keeping its `Context`. What the
front end lost is the thread; what the engine gained is the job.

**The plan was wrong about one thing, and Fact 1 is why.** It said this stage
would first use `Repaint` for a real tick, turning `spoken.rs`'s three
`request_repaint_after(TICK)` calls into `repaint.after(TICK)`. It cannot: those
three calls and the `Waker` built in `open` are *all six* of the file's toolkit
lines, so converting them makes `spoken.rs` toolkit-free — and a toolkit-free
file that stays in `ui-egui` is trapped, taking the count 10 → 11 and reddening
the gate. `Spoken` cannot move down while it returns `Lyrics` and `View`. So the
`Repaint` conversion of `spoken.rs` has to wait for the `Lyrics`/`View` split,
and this stage took the thread instead.

**Two constraints handled.**

- `read_aloud/mod.rs` is at 1933 of a 1933 ceiling and refers to
  `spoken::Start` in three places, so it could not absorb an import-path edit.
  `spoken.rs` re-exports instead — one `pub use` line — and `mod.rs` is
  **untouched**. `spoken.rs` had shrunk to 932 against a 1106 ceiling, so the
  line was free.
- Five imports were orphaned by the move and had to go: `SyncSender`, `Mutex`,
  `TextLayer`, `Glyph`, `Script`. Two more look used but are not — `BTreeMap`
  survives only as the fully-qualified `std::collections::BTreeMap` in two field
  types, and `Sentence` only as the *variant* `Highlight::Sentence`. A word-boundary
  grep counts both as live; reading the lines does not. All seven were dropped.

**Three engine tests**, using `crates/engine/src/sample.rs` — "documents made in
memory, so a test can read one without a file" — opened synchronously the way
`paper.rs` and `texts.rs` do it, with `?` in a `Result`-returning test rather
than a `panic!` the engine denies. `a_thread_reads_the_sample_and_wakes_the_window`
is the exit gate's shape: it starts the job, drains the channel until the thread
drops its sender, and asserts both that the `Waker` fired and that page 0's
length was counted. The drain cannot hang, because a thread that fails to start
drops the sender at once.

**Gate effect, measured at `8a2c46b`:** thread-spawning files in the front end
**15 → 14**;
trapped holds at **10** (`spoken.rs` still names egui, so it was never counted);
the engine stays toolkit-free; `spoken.rs` **1098 → 932** against its 1106
ceiling, left unrecorded since the repo re-records ceilings in their own commit.

### Stage 5b — the `Lyrics`/`View` split, which is what stage 5 deferred

`Spoken`'s remaining toolkit lines are six, and all six are a `Context`. It
cannot cross while it returns `super::card::Lyrics` and `super::control::View`
and takes `control::Act`, so this is the interleaving Fact 2 describes, and it is
a real split rather than a move: the state and the sentence logic go down, and
`lyrics()`, `view()` and `act()` become view-side functions over what is left.
Until then `spoken.rs` keeps its `Context`, and its three `TICK` waits stay in
the view.

### Stage 6 — the `page_ai` family (six files, 4,235 lines)

The largest single win: three thread files (`groups.rs`, `follow.rs`, `cards.rs`)
and eight of the fourteen `ctx.input(|i| i.time)` clock reads.

- `groups.rs` (457, four toolkit lines) needs only `now: f64` and a `Waker`.
- `follow.rs` (508, seven) needs those plus `Repaint` for its three waits.
- `mod.rs` (1,687, thirteen) holds `PageAi`'s state: `batches`, `runs`, `showing`.
- `menu.rs` (804) is view — pointer and key input, `ctx.input_mut`.
- **`cards.rs` cannot move.** Lines 212-214 build an `egui::ColorImage` and call
  `ctx.load_texture`; limit 5 ("no pixels below L6") keeps that up. It splits.

**Corrected at `b8e3966`, which did the split.** The blocker was not the thread —
`worker()` never touches a `Context` — but what it *sends*: `Set` carried its
geometry as egui's `Vec2` and `Rect`. `reflow` had already solved it, because
`Flow::pictures` and `Flow::emojis` return `Vec<[f32; 4]>` and `set` turned each
into a `Rect` with one closure on its last line. `Set` now carries reflow's own
arrays and a `(f32, f32)` size; that one closure moved up into `upload`, where the
pixels were already being handed to the GPU. The arithmetic is the same expression
over the same values and runs after the channel instead of before it, so `Card`
keeps `Vec2` and `Rect` unchanged and `paint.rs` is untouched. `Job`, `Ask`,
`Done`, `Set`, `worker`, `flow_of`, `fit`, `part` and `set` went down; `Cards`,
`Card`, `upload`, `on_white` and `KEEP` stayed. No dependency moved, so the lock
was quiet.
- `paint.rs` (452) is view by definition.

`engine::page_ai` already exists (`mod.rs` 71 lines of types, `extract.rs` 339),
so the engine half has a home.

**Gate effect:** thread files **14 → 11**; trapped unchanged unless
`library`/`trigger` move here.

## Verification, and its cost

Mode is **`ci`** on the owner's instruction: nothing compiles here. `ci.py mode`
recommends `cache` and the sandbox cache is warm and current for this branch, but
the owner's Rule 1 overrides detection, so no `cargo` runs locally.

What runs here, and has never disagreed with CI: `shape_check.py --dups`,
`comment_check.py`, `comment_budget.py`, `commit_check.py`, and hand-rolled
replicas of `trapped` and the engine toolkit grep.

What only CI can see: unused imports, derives, borrows, anything under
`-D warnings`. Two red runs so far were exactly that — `#[derive(Debug)]` on a
struct holding a `Waker` (`dyn Fn()` is not `Debug`), and an unused `FULL_TINT`.

**The discipline that keeps the cache alive:** push once, wait for the CI run to
*complete*, wait for `Cache / sandbox` to *complete*, and only then push again.
`concurrency: ci-${ref}-${event_name}` with `cancel-in-progress: true` means a
new push cancels the in-flight one, and `refresh` only fires `needs: check` on
success — so pushing early cancels the job that builds the cache. Cold ≈ 17 min,
warm ≈ 5½ min.

Stage sizes are chosen to fit that loop: a stage is one closed set, one push, one
verdict. Nothing is batched across stages, because a red run then names two
stages' worth of failures at once.
