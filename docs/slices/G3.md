### Why `Repaint` exists

Law 5 sends a wake out through `Waker = Arc<dyn Fn() + Send + Sync>`, which is
all a worker that *finishes* needs. A controller that polls an engine only
answering when asked, or waits for a settle time to arrive, has to say **when**,
and a closure taking nothing cannot. So those wait in the view, and that is the
only thing pinning three files to a `Context`:

| File | Line | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read_aloud/booth.rs` | 177 | `ctx.request_repaint_after(TICK)` — some systems only report a finished sample when polled |
| `read_aloud/spoken.rs` | 658, 676, 695 | the same `TICK` poll, three places |
| `library/folders.rs` | 263 | `request_repaint_after(at - now)` — wake when a rescan's settle time arrives |
| `page_ai/follow.rs` | 297, 313, 327 | a state machine's dwell and retry waits |

`Repaint` sits **beside** `Waker` rather than replacing it: the review fixes that
alias, and `speech`, `assist`, `dict` and `cefr` all export it, so it cannot take
an argument. One closure taking a `Duration`, zero for at once.

## Part two — the three facts that dictate the plan

These were measured, and each one rules out an approach that looks obvious.

### Fact 1 — trapped has zero headroom, so nothing may be freed in place

`tools/trapped_count.txt` is **10** and the count is **10**. The gate fails when
`found > ceiling`, so:

> **Any commit that makes a `ui-egui` file toolkit-free without moving it into
> `crates/engine` in the same commit turns CI red.**

That rules out the natural incremental plan — "first decouple every controller
from `Context`, then move the freed files down". Concretely: converting
`spoken.rs` to `Repaint` removes all six of its toolkit lines, and the commit
that does it must also move the file, or trapped goes 10 → 11.

It also means P3 cannot go one file at a time where files import each other.
Each stage has to move a **closed set**.

### Fact 2 — the coupling is tiny; the interleaving is the problem

Toolkit lines against file size, for the review's in-scope files:

```
  7 toolkit lines / 1309 total   study/mod.rs
  6 toolkit lines / 1106 total   read_aloud/spoken.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  457 total   page_ai/groups.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  399 total   word_list/mod.rs
  4 toolkit lines /  408 total   library/io.rs
 15 toolkit lines / 1727 total   documents.rs
 56 toolkit lines / 1933 total   read_aloud/mod.rs
```

Decoupling is hours of mechanical work. What is hard is that the engine half is
*interleaved* with the view half, so a file cannot be cut cleanly:

- `spoken.rs` imports `super::card::Lyrics` (13 toolkit words) and
  `super::control::{Act, View}` (36), and `ReadAloud` holds a
  `Session::Spoken(Box<Spoken>)` variant — so `Spoken` is pinned by the card's
  lyrics and the control's view.
- `page_ai` shares `Pill`/`Ring` (`paint.rs`) and `Around`/`Asked` across all six
  of its files.
- the trapped `library/{actions,organizing}.rs` import `super::LibraryState` and
  `crate::ui_command::{Commands, Sent, UiCommand}`.

This is the same wall G2 recorded for `PdfView`: *"59 match sites rewriting what
a `PdfView` is, and with no compiler that is a change I could not verify."*

### Fact 3 — `Desk` says exactly what is left, and who owns it

move"*. Its 51 methods, grouped by the controller the adapter reaches

## The plan

Ordered so that every stage is a closed set: nothing is freed without being
moved, and no stage depends on a later one. Each stage ends with the Python
gates green here, one push, and one CI verdict.

### Stage 4 — `Booth`, `TICK` and `tone` cross (the one genuinely free piece)

`booth.rs` was 219 lines with **four** toolkit lines, and all four were a
`Context`: building a `Waker` in `start`, threading `ctx` through `open` and
`try_voice_of`, and the `TICK` poll in `tick`. After `Repaint` it names no
toolkit at all.

Its only two upward dependencies were small and both crossed with it:

- `super::TICK` — `const TICK: Duration = Duration::from_millis(50)`, in
  `read_aloud/mod.rs:39`.
- `super::spoken::tone` — five lines, `ReadAloudSettings` → `mareader_speech::Tone`,
  no toolkit, no view type. It joins `TICK` in `engine::read_aloud`'s root,
  beside `ReadFrom`, because both `Booth` and `spoken.rs` want it.

**What the write-set turned out to be.** New `crates/engine/src/read_aloud/booth.rs`
(357 lines with its tests); `TICK` and `tone` into `engine/src/read_aloud/mod.rs`;
`ui-egui/src/read_aloud/booth.rs` deleted; `ui-egui/src/read_aloud/mod.rs`
re-points `pub use Booth` at the engine and gains `attach_repaint`; `spoken.rs`
names the engine's `TICK` and `tone` where it used to name its own; and the four
callers — `app/read_aloud.rs`, `app/frame/chrome.rs`, `app/glosses.rs`,
`app/apply/desk.rs` — pass one argument fewer.

**The shape that mattered: attach once, not per call.** The obvious edit is
`ctx` → `&Repaint` in each signature. But `voices_shown` runs *every frame* while
a voice tab shows, so `repaint::of(ctx)` at the call site would allocate an `Arc`

## Verification, and its cost

Mode is **`ci`** on the owner's instruction: nothing compiles here. `ci.py mode`
recommends `cache` and the sandbox cache is warm and current for this branch, but
the owner's Rule 1 overrides detection, so no `cargo` runs locally.

What runs here, and has never disagreed with CI: `shape_check.py --dups`,
`comment_check.py`, `comment_budget.py`, `commit_check.py`, and hand-rolled
replicas of `trapped` and the engine toolkit grep.

What only CI can see: unused imports, derives, borrows, anything under
`-D warnings`. Two red runs so far were exactly that — `#[derive(Debug)]` on a
struct holding a `Waker` (`dyn Fn()` is not `Debug`), and an unused `FULL_TINT`.

**The discipline that keeps the cache alive:** push once, wait for the CI run to
*complete*, wait for `Cache / sandbox` to *complete*, and only then push again.
`concurrency: ci-${ref}-${event_name}` with `cancel-in-progress: true` means a
new push cancels the in-flight one, and `refresh` only fires `needs: check` on
success — so pushing early cancels the job that builds the cache. Cold ≈ 17 min,
warm ≈ 5½ min.

Stage sizes are chosen to fit that loop: a stage is one closed set, one push, one
verdict. Nothing is batched across stages, because a red run then names two
stages' worth of failures at once.
